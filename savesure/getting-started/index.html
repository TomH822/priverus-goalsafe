Getting Started with SaveSure

Some expenses arrive once or twice a year and always seem to catch us short: property taxes, insurance, the holidays. SaveSure helps you have that money waiting in your savings account when the day comes, without guessing how much to set aside.

No bank connection. Your data stays on your device.

How it works

All your money stays in one savings account. SaveSure divides it, on paper, into funds:

  • The Primary Fund covers your must-pay withdrawals. SaveSure works out one steady, even deposit that keeps it on schedule.
  • Reserve Funds are goals you rank by priority, such as a Christmas Club, Family Birthdays or Home Repair.

A quick example

Say you have $1,200 in property taxes due in December, $600 of car insurance in March, and a $400 summer trip. You list those three planned withdrawals with their dates, and tell SaveSure how often you add money to savings. SaveSure then shows the steady amount each deposit needs to cover them all on time.

Setting up

A short setup guide walks you through it:

  1. Choose your start date and enter today's savings balance.
  2. List your planned withdrawals: name, amount and date.
  3. Set your regular deposit and how often it happens. SaveSure checks that it's enough.
  4. Choose whether you'd like reminders before withdrawals.
  5. Add any Reserve Funds, rank them, and divide your starting balance among your funds.
  6. Read the disclaimer, tick the box, and finish. Home then tells you where you stand.

What Home shows you

  • On Track! or Needs Attention, at a glance.
  • Your SaveSure Balance beside the Balance at Bank you enter by hand, so you can check that they agree.
  • Your next deposit, and how much of it the Primary Fund needs.
  • Your Primary Fund, with details one tap away.
  • Your Reserve Funds in priority order, each showing how much it holds toward its target. A fund that falls behind is marked.
  • Free to spend or move: money not set aside for any fund.
  • Your next withdrawal.

A notice appears only when something needs your attention. SaveSure never connects to your bank, so you record deposits and withdrawals yourself. The Projections tab charts your funds month by month.

Good to know

  • After you check your bank, update the Balance at Bank so the two figures can be compared.
  • Add or change a planned withdrawal at any time, and SaveSure recalculates your deposit.
  • Banner ads can be removed with a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase, which you can restore on a new device.
